What this panel is for

Read-only model assessment - scoring and auditing the model as a whole, rather than changing anything in it.

These two report on model quality: Model Health Check scores it against 12 metrics, and Model Checker runs a customizable rule-based compliance check. Anything that lists and edits model content lives in the Manage panel instead; finding in-place families to convert or remove moved to the Clean up panel.

SCORE

Model Health Check

Scores the model against 12 metrics and shows a colour-coded dashboard with per-metric recommendations.

 COMPLIANCE

Model Checker

Runs a rule-based BIM standards check (project settings, naming conventions, performance) against a customizable checkset.
