What this panel is for

Model housekeeping - removing unused family content and elements you no longer need - with a scan step before anything is deleted.

Three tools cover different scopes: Purge Families cleans up a whole folder of .rfa files on disk, Smart Delete removes specific elements you pick after checking what depends on them, and In-Place Model Manager finds in-place families worth converting to a real loadable family or removing outright.

ON DISK

Purge Families

Batch-purge every .rfa in a folder until Revit reports nothing purgeable left, with an optional bulk rename pass.

 TARGETED

Smart Delete

Pick specific elements and see what depends on them before you delete - flags anything that isn't safe to remove.

 FAMILIES

In-Place Model Manager

List every in-place family with geometry complexity and quality flags, to find conversion/deletion candidates.
